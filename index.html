I am a final-year undergraduate in Engineering Physics at IIT Madras, currently a Research Intern at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University under Prof. Howie Choset.

I enjoy thinking about motion planning and control from a geometric perspective — how structure in a problem can be exploited to design more efficient and reliable algorithms. I am also interested in the intersection of these ideas with data-driven methods.
