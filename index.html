B.Tech in Engineering Physics

Indian Institute of Technology Madras

I am a final-year BTech student in Engineering Physics at Indian Institute of Technology Madras.

My work sits at the intersection of geometric mechanics, motion planning, and control. I am drawn to approaches that are mathematically principled — where structure in a problem is exploited to design algorithms that are not just empirically effective, but provably well-behaved. I care about the full arc from theory to hardware: rigorous foundations that hold up under real physics, not just clean simulations.

I am particularly interested in safety-critical robotics and the integration of geometric and data-driven methods for planning and control in complex environments. I am looking to pursue a PhD in robotics where I can develop methods that are both theoretically grounded and practically deployed.
